## News Outlets

### National News

- [Tagesschau](https://www.tagesschau.de/) - Germany's leading news program and website (ARD).
- [ZDF heute](https://www.heute.de/) - News portal from Germany's public broadcaster ZDF.
- [Deutsche Welle (DW)](https://www.dw.com/de/) - International broadcaster with news in German and 30+ languages.
- [Süddeutsche Zeitung (SZ)](https://www.sueddeutsche.de/) - One of Germany's most respected daily newspapers.
- [Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung (FAZ)](https://www.faz.net/) - Leading German newspaper for politics and economics.
- [Die Zeit](https://www.zeit.de/) - Weekly newspaper known for in-depth journalism.
- [Der Spiegel](https://www.spiegel.de/) - Germany's leading news magazine.
- [Bild](https://www.bild.de/) - Germany's largest tabloid newspaper.
- [Focus](https://www.focus.de/) - News magazine covering current affairs.
- [Taz (die tageszeitung)](https://taz.de/) - Left-leaning daily newspaper.
- [Welt](https://www.welt.de/) - Conservative daily newspaper.
- [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/) - Leading German business newspaper.

### Regional News

- [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/) - Berlin regional newspaper.
- [Hamburger Abendblatt](https://www.abendblatt.de/) - Hamburg regional news.
- [Münchner Merkur](https://www.merkur.de/) - Munich and Bavaria regional news.
- [Kölner Stadt-Anzeiger](https://www.ksta.de/) - Cologne regional newspaper.
- [Stuttgarter Zeitung](https://www.stuttgarter-zeitung.de/) - Stuttgart regional news.
- [Rheinische Post](https://rp-online.de/) - Düsseldorf and North Rhine-Westphalia news.

### Business & Finance

- [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/) - Germany's premier business daily.
- [Wirtschaftswoche](https://www.wiwo.de/) - Business and finance weekly magazine.
- [Manager Magazin](https://www.manager-magazin.de/) - Business magazine focusing on management.
- [Börsen-Zeitung](https://www.boersen-zeitung.de/) - Financial markets newspaper.
- [Finanz und Wirtschaft](https://www.fuw.ch/) - Swiss-German financial newspaper.

### Technology

- [Heise Online](https://www.heise.de/) - Leading German tech news portal.
- [Golem.de](https://www.golem.de/) - Technology and IT news.
- [t3n](https://t3n.de/) - Digital business and tech magazine.
- [Netzpolitik.org](https://netzpolitik.org/) - Digital rights and tech policy news.
- [Computerbild](https://www.computerbild.de/) - Consumer technology magazine.

### Sports

- [Kicker](https://www.kicker.de/) - Germany's leading sports magazine, especially football.
- [Sport1](https://www.sport1.de/) - Multi-sport news portal.
- [Sportschau](https://www.sportschau.de/) - ARD's sports news platform.
- [Eurosport.de](https://www.eurosport.de/) - International sports news in German.

## Reading Practice Tools

Tools and resources specifically designed for German language learners reading news:

- [Nachrichtenleicht](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/) - News in simplified German (Deutschlandfunk).
- [Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten (DW)](https://www.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/nachrichten/s-8030) - Slowly spoken news for learners.
- [Deutsch Perfekt](https://www.deutsch-perfekt.com/) - Magazine for German learners with current affairs.
- [Top-Thema mit Vokabeln (DW)](https://www.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/top-thema/s-8031) - Weekly news topics with vocabulary.
- [Lingua.ly](https://lingua.ly/) - Browser extension for reading practice with integrated dictionary.
- [Readlang](https://readlang.com/) - Web reader tool for foreign language practice.
- [News in Slow German](https://www.newsinslowgerman.com/) - Weekly news podcast for learners.
- [German-English Dictionary App](https://german-english-dictionary.devtheworld.jp/) - For scientifically-efficient modern learning.
